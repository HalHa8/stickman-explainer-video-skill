# Basketball example

Use this reference only as an example of a confirmed analogy and a dense but readable storyboard. Do not make basketball concepts universal.

## Confirmed mapping

- Agent: a player who observes, judges, and decides.
- Tool: an atomic basketball action such as dribbling, accelerating, passing, gathering, or shooting.
- Skill: one player's compound move made from tools in a defined sequence.
- Agent combining skills: for example, use a between-the-legs drive and then a pull-up jumper.
- Multi-agent: several thinking players coordinate toward winning; screens, rolls, cuts, spacing, and rebounding belong here when multiple players are involved.

## Useful visual patterns

- Opening shot reference: narrate `如果把AI放到篮球场，Agent、Tool、Skill和MultiAgent，其实一下就能听懂。` Show `如果把AI放到篮球场？` at the top of the safe content region, then reveal `Agent`, `Tool`, `Skill`, and `Multi-agent` one by one as they are spoken. End with all four visible. Keep the physical top 10% blank.
- Four-panel tool shot: show the same player performing four actions. Keep all panels visible and light them in spoken order. When shortening, cut the quiet opening so the final highlight and ellipsis remain readable.
- Skill decomposition: show a move card beside the atomic-action icons that compose it.
- Agent composition: connect two complete skill cards and show one player executing both.
- Multi-agent transition: begin with one player outnumbered, pull back, then introduce teammates and coordination lines.
- Summary: reveal Agent, Tool, Skill, and Multi-agent cards in narration order.

## Text and speech spelling

- Display `sub-agent` and `Multi-agent` with hyphens when that is the chosen editorial style.
- Feed `SubAgent` and `MultiAgent` to TTS when the engine otherwise inserts an audible pause.

The source project for this example used a blue lead player, red defenders, an orange ball, a white background, a top 10% safe area, concept-only screen text, and no subtitle track.
